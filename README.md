## 📌 Project Overview
Built an end-to-end HR Analytics & Workforce Attrition dashboard in Power BI analyzing workforce demographics, compensation bands, overtime patterns, and retention factors across **400+ employee records**.

The primary objective was to diagnose the root causes of employee turnover, evaluate burnout risk factors, and empower HR leadership with actionable retention strategies.

---

## 🎯 Key Metrics & Insights
- **Baseline Attrition Rate:** Quantified an overall turnover rate of **30.5%**.
- **Overtime Correlation:** Identified a clear correlation between consistent overtime hours and elevated attrition risk in technical and operational roles.
- **Salary Binning & Distribution:** Clustered employee compensation into distinct salary brackets to uncover pay-disparity drivers across job levels.
- **Burnout Matrix Heatmap:** Developed a multi-variable matrix combining overtime frequency, department load, and tenure to identify flight-risk teams.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Implementation & DAX Measures
- **Data Modeling:** Cleaned and structured 400+ raw employee records; established dimension tables and relationships.
- **Custom DAX Measures:** Authored calculations for dynamic attrition rates, headcount changes, salary bin categorizations, and conditional metric flags.
- **Interactive UI/UX:** Built dynamic slicers (Department, Role, Overtime status, Age bands) and synchronized cross-filtering across all visual cards.
